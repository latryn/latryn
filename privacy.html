Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026.

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

What we collect

When you use the contact form, your email app opens with a message addressed to us. We only receive what you choose to send, such as your name, email address, company and message.

When you apply to the Lattice, we receive the answers you enter (name, email, phone number, education, specialization, type of work, availability and any profile link) and the resume you upload. They are stored in our Google Workspace account (Google Sheets and Google Drive), and we send a confirmation to the email address you give.

How we use it

We use your message to reply to you and to discuss a possible project or role. We use applications only to assess you for the Lattice and to contact you about it. We do not sell your information or share it for marketing.

Retention and your rights

We keep correspondence for as long as needed to respond and to meet legal obligations. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing contact@latryn.com.

Third parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives standard request information such as your IP address. Lattice applications are processed by Google Workspace on our behalf.
